01 · Research

Selected Research

My current work covers two related areas: visual representation under appearance changes and scientific reasoning with structured constraints.

01 Co-first author (listed first) · ECCV 2026 accepted 2025.10 - 2026.03

Reliability-Aware 3D Geometric Injection for Universal Person Re-identification

Occlusion, clothing changes, and cross-modality make appearance cues less reliable. UniGeo introduces monocular 3D body geometry as a complementary cue and uses a reliability-aware gate to limit the effect of low-confidence geometry estimates. We conducted comparisons, ablations, and perturbation-stability tests on nine benchmarks, including Market-1501 and MSMT17.

2D appearance + 3D geometry → Reliability gate → Identity representation

02 First Author · WAICA Workshop Accepted 2026.03 - present

PEARL: Verifiable Curation and Small-Model Learning for Scientific Reasoning Graph Extraction

A scientific reasoning graph must be semantically meaningful while also satisfying structural, compilation, and source-alignment constraints. PEARL separates model-generated candidates from code-based checks. The preliminary version validated this workflow; the full version uses curated graphs to train smaller models and is being extended toward an AAAI submission.

Candidate graphs → Code-based checks → Curated training data
Workshop version accepted 350-sample evaluation Full version in progress
03 First author · In progress · Targeting TPAMI 2026.03 - present

Appearance Variations in Person Re-Identification: A Survey

Existing ReID studies often discuss pose, occlusion, clothing changes, cross-modality, and long-term deployment separately, although their boundaries are not always clear. This survey reorganizes the field around short-term perturbations, long-term appearance changes, and compound variations, and reviews methods and evaluation through the distinction between appearance robustness and appearance generalization. Model fine-tuning and testing are also in progress.

Short-term perturbations / Long-term changes / Compound variations

02 · Systems

Selected Projects

These projects span smartphone agents, embedded communication, robotics, and mathematical modeling, with work across system design, algorithm development, and engineering implementation.

National Second Prize 2025.05 - 2025.08

A4R: Guided Smartphone AI Assistant

I co-designed a clarify-execute-verify interaction workflow and built more than 150 real interactions across six domains and 16 applications. On China Unicom tasks, the system achieved a 64.68% completion rate and a 72.33% effective-guidance rate.

National First Prize · Top 6 2023.11 - 2025.08

Modular Robot System

I was responsible for module coordination, bus arbitration, hot-plug discovery, and bus merging, allowing the controller to rediscover topology and restore communication after modules were added or reordered. The system reached a 98.5% communication success rate and improved communication efficiency by about 40% over the original design.

MCM 2026 Finalist 2026.01 - 2026.02

SmartPhone Battery: The Moment of Depletion

Our team modeled screen, computation, and communication loads in a shared physical framework, then cross-checked an ECM-EKF solver with a physics-informed neural network. Across eight data sources and more than 120,000 time steps, SOC RMSE was 1.24% and overall TTE MAPE was 8.7%.

Xiaomi Cup National Third Prize 2025.03 - 2025.08

Xiaomi Quadruped Logistics System

I contributed to system design, algorithm debugging, workflow optimization, and on-site testing. My work focused on state transitions and action timing between delivery stages, reducing the downstream effects of recognition failures and path deviations.
